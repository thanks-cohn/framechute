Prototype scope and next steps

This standalone prototype uses the freely licensed town artwork in your uploaded asset pack. You can move through one town, use two marked exits, explore a small overworld, and return to the same town from its corresponding entrances. The eastern town on the overworld is a future desktop placeholder, not a functioning second desktop. Actual SUBSTRATE frame and document switching, ship controls, sky transitions, and globe mode come later.
